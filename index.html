01 / THE PHILOSOPHY OF MA

I don’t just paint.
I sculpt the silence between forms.

Rooted in the Kyoto concept of Ma—the charged stillness between physical objects—each canvas and monoprint investigates what remains unpainted.

Dense passages of crushed Badakhshan slate, pine soot sumi, and raw bone glue collide with unbleached Echizen washi fibers. The work invites the viewer into an contemplative space where shadows carry emotional presence.

Direct Studio Inquiry

Original oil and sumi canvases are available through direct studio inquiry or during scheduled gallery exhibitions. For private viewing appointments at our London atelier or to discuss bespoke architectural commissions, please contact studio@chintwo.com.

Yes. Every limited edition is hand-pulled on museum-grade 300gsm Somerset Velvet cotton rag or handmade Echizen kozo paper. Each impression is hand-inspected, numbered, blind-stamped with the Chintwo studio seal, and accompanied by a signed Certificate of Authenticity.

We ship worldwide using specialized fine-art couriers with climate-controlled crates, full transit insurance, and white-glove delivery options for large-format canvases across Europe, the Americas, and Asia.
