01 / BIOGRAPHY & VISION

About me

Hey, I’m Chintwo, a visual artist and storyteller based between London and Kyoto. I focus on monumental oil canvases, atmospheric chiaroscuro compositions, and limited archival editions, exploring the emotional resonance of solitude.

My goal is simple: I sculpt real stillness that feels like you. I want you to feel the quiet breathing room between forms, whether it is an original commission for a private collection or a museum installation.

ORIGINS

Japanese Washi Roots & Echizen Craft

My formative studio apprenticeship in the Echizen valley shaped everything I understand about surface tension. There, fourth-generation papermakers cook raw mulberry bark in mountain spring water, letting winter chill tighten the fibers before pressing each sheet against warm wood.

When sumi ink touches this unbleached fiber, it does not sit atop a canvas—it absorbs into the living history of the plant. That tactile resistance remains the foundation of every piece produced in our London and Kyoto workspaces.

ATELIER RITUAL • NIKAWA SIZING & IWA-ENOGU GRINDING KYOTO

PHILOSOPHY

The Architecture of Silence (Ma)

In Western composition, silence is often feared as empty space requiring decoration. In Japanese aesthetics, the concept of Ma recognizes space as active, pregnant with potential, and resonant.

By withholding details and allowing deep chiaroscuro shadows to dominate large passages of a canvas, the composition asks the viewer to slow down, breathe, and bring their own emotional memory into the unfinished interval.

ATELIER GEAR & TOOLS

My gear and substrates

KYOTO • FUKUI • LONDON

01 / SUBSTRATE

Echizen Kozo Washi

Hand-formed mulberry bark papers cooked in spring water, unbleached, sun-dried on ginkgo boards in Fukui prefecture.

02 / PIGMENT

Sumi Ink Sticks (Kobaien)

Kiln-collected pine soot pressed with animal gelatin, aged between 5 and 30 years in Nara cedar chests to impart deep matte blacks.

03 / MINERAL COLOR

Natural Mineral Pigments (Iwa-enogu)

Finely crushed malachite, azurite, cinnabar, and powdered oyster shell (gofun) bound with warm cowhide nikawa glue.

04 / STRUCTURE

Belgian Unprimed Linen

Heavyweight 450gsm pure flax linen stretched over custom tulipwood expansion bars to support heavy slate glazes.
